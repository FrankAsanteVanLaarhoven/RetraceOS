# RETRACE

Frank Asante Van Laarhoven is the credited author. The license is Apache-2.0.

The verifier is the only component that may assign `REPRODUCED_WITHIN_CONTRACT`.
A repair worker, a notebook cell, and a prompt must not change a result contract,
a reference output, a tolerance, an approval, or the verifier. Approval is bound
to the candidate hash, the contract hash, and the snapshot hash.

Unknown notebooks stay unexecuted. Do not add a host fallback when a sandbox is
absent. Demonstration fixtures are labelled injected faults, not defects in
published scientific software.

Do not claim production readiness, row-level security, a tested sandbox, a full
RO-Crate certification, or a completed human-participant study from this tree.
Connector credentials that are missing stay `NEEDS_CONFIGURATION`.
Model keys are not called by the deterministic diagnoser.
